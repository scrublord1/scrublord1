Privacy Policy

YT Psych Uploader · Effective 26 October 2026

What this app is

YT Psych Uploader is a private tool operated by one YouTube creator to manage their own channel. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data it accesses

  • Videos, metadata and comments on the authorising creator's own channel.
  • Analytics for that channel (views, watch time, click-through rate).

It does not access any other user's account, private messages, or payment information.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload the creator's videos, reply to comments on those videos, and decide which topics perform best. OAuth tokens and channel data are stored locally on the creator's own computer and are not sold or shared for advertising.

Public comment text from the creator's videos may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API solely to help draft replies. No other third parties receive data.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data is deleted when the creator removes the app's folder.
